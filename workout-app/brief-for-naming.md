# Naming brief — AI Workout / Virtual Personal Trainer app

## What we're naming

A mobile app (iOS first, Android close behind) that acts as a **virtual personal
trainer**. The core experience:

- The user inputs their **goal** (lose fat, build muscle, marathon prep, general
  health, sport-specific, etc.), their **current physical state** (height,
  weight, training history, injuries, available equipment, time per week), and
  their **constraints** (gym vs. home, dietary preferences, schedule).
- An **AI model generates a personalized training program** — split, exercise
  selection, sets, reps, load progression, deload weeks, the lot.
- The program **adapts over time** based on logged sessions, perceived effort,
  performance trends, missed days, and user feedback.
- Stretch features (later): nutrition guidance, voice coaching during workouts,
  form check via camera, recovery and sleep integration.

Think: ChatGPT + a thoughtful S&C coach in your pocket. Not a static
program library. Not a generic workout tracker.

## Target audience

- Primary: people who would benefit from a personal trainer but can't afford
  one (~$80–150/session) or don't have access locally. Age 22–45, both genders,
  some prior gym experience but not advanced.
- Secondary: experienced lifters who want programming offloaded.
- Geography: Nordic market first (Norway), then English-speaking globally.
  Name should work in both Norwegian and English without translation.

## Brand qualities to optimize for

| Quality | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Easy to spell** | Word-of-mouth, App Store search, voice queries. No homophone traps (e.g. "Sensai" → "Sensei"). |
| **Easy to pronounce** | Should sound the same in Norwegian, English, German. No ambiguous stress patterns. |
| **Short** | 5–7 characters ideal, 8 max. Fits in app icons, social handles, domain. |
| **Memorable / brandable** | Distinct enough that a single mention sticks. Not generic ("FitPro", "GymBuddy"). |
| **Fitness-resonant but not literal** | Hints at strength/training without being "Fit-X" or "Train-X" generic. Invented words and mythological roots have historically scored highest. |
| **AI / modern feel** | This is an AI product. Should feel 2026, not 2010 boot-camp app. |
| **No negative connotations** | No slurs, no medical/pharma associations, no political loading. Must clear scrutiny in EN, NO, DE, ES at minimum. |
