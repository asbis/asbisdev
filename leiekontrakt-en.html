3. Duration and Termination of the Tenancy

3.1 Type and duration

The tenancy is for a fixed term. The tenancy begins on 01.07.2026 and ends on 31.12.2026 without prior notice.

If the Tenant does not move out by the end date of the tenancy, the Landlord must send the Tenant a written request to vacate within three months after the end date. If this is not done, the tenancy will be converted into an open-ended (indefinite) tenancy.

3.2 Notice of termination

The tenancy has a 2-month notice period, ending at the close of a calendar month.

The tenancy runs as normal throughout the notice period, and during this period the parties shall maintain their obligations under the agreement. This means that the Tenant has the right to use the premises and is obliged to pay rent throughout the notice period. The Landlord, for their part, is obliged to make the premises available to the Tenant throughout the notice period.

4. The Rent

The rent is NOK 7,500 per month.

The following is in addition to or included in the rent:

  • Internet is included in the rent.
  • Cable TV is included in the rent.
  • Water and sewage are included in the rent.
  • Electricity/heating is included in the rent.

The total amount is paid on the 1st of each month for the current calendar month. The amount shall be paid to bank account 2901.21.32482 unless the Tenant is informed otherwise.

5. Adjustment of the Rent

With one month's written notice, either party may require the rent to be adjusted in line with changes in the consumer price index since the rent was last set. An adjustment may take effect at the earliest 12 months after the rent was last set.

If the tenancy has lasted at least two years and six months without any rent adjustment other than according to the consumer price index, either party may, with six months' written notice, require the rent to be set to the prevailing market rent (gjengsleie), cf. the Norwegian Tenancy Act § 4-3.

6. Standard and Handover of the Rental Object

The rental object, which is furnished, is taken over in its current condition. At handover, the rental object and its accessories shall be tidied, cleaned and in normal good condition.

Upon taking over the rental object, the Tenant will be given keys. The Tenant may not make copies of additional keys to the rental object without the Landlord's consent.

The Tenant is encouraged to inspect the rental object in advance. The rental object is let in the condition it is in at the viewing, cf. the Norwegian Tenancy Act § 2-5. A defect in the rental object will exist if the rental object is in significantly poorer condition than the Tenant had reason to expect under the circumstances, or if incorrect or missing information was given about the rental object and this is assumed to have affected the agreement. The deadline for asserting defects is within a reasonable time after the defect was discovered, cf. the Norwegian Tenancy Act § 2-8. If the Tenant does not give the required notice within the deadline, any potential claim for compensation may be reduced or lost.

The Tenant has attended a viewing and has thereby become acquainted with the size, standard and layout of the rental object.

7. Obligations of the Parties During the Tenancy

7.1 The Tenant's obligations

The Tenant is obliged to maintain door locks, taps, toilets, electrical sockets and switches, water heaters, and fixtures and equipment in the premises that are not part of the real property. The Tenant is also obliged to carry out necessary function checks, cleaning, battery changes, testing and the like of smoke detectors and fire-extinguishing equipment. If maintenance is not cost-effective, replacement is the responsibility of the Landlord. Repair of accidental damage is not regarded as maintenance.

Without the Landlord's prior written consent, the Tenant may not make structural alterations or new fittings. Without such consent, the Tenant may not paint, change wall coverings, flooring or the like.

The Tenant is obliged to immediately notify the Landlord of any damage requiring urgent repair by the Landlord. Other damage must be reported without undue delay.

The Tenant is obliged at all times to hold ordinary home contents and personal property insurance. The Landlord may require the insurance certificate to be presented.

7.2 The Landlord's obligations

The Landlord is obliged to make the rental object with the agreed accessories available to the Tenant throughout the rental period. The Landlord shall keep the rental object and the property in the condition agreed or as otherwise follows from the provisions of the Norwegian Tenancy Act. If the Landlord breaches these obligations, the Tenant may invoke the remedies in Chapter 2 and § 5-7 of the Tenancy Act.

8. House Rules

The Tenant is obliged to maintain normal peace and order on the property.

The Tenant is obliged to follow ordinary rules of order and reasonable instructions established by the Landlord to ensure good order in the house, and any rules of order etc. that at any time apply to the housing cooperative or jointly owned property.

Pets on the property are not permitted.

Smoking in the rental object is not permitted.

9. Subletting and Taking In Members of the Household

The Tenant may not sublet or in any other way transfer their right of use to others, or take persons into their household, without the Landlord's consent, unless otherwise required by mandatory law. Consent may be refused at any time if the premises would clearly become overcrowded.

10. Transfer of the Tenancy

The Tenant may not transfer their rights or obligations under the tenancy agreement to others without the Landlord's consent, unless otherwise required by mandatory law.

11. Termination of the Tenancy – Return of the Premises, etc.

On moving out, the Tenant shall return the rental object, including fixed and loose equipment, in well-maintained condition (and in the same condition as at takeover, except for the deterioration caused by ordinary wear and tear, and the defects the Landlord is obliged to repair), unless otherwise agreed.

The Tenant shall, at their own expense, ensure proper cleaning before vacating the rental object, including floors, walls, ceilings, cupboards and windows.

If the Tenant, with the Landlord's consent, has made significant improvements to the premises, the Tenant may claim compensation for the benefit the Landlord gains as a result of the improvements, unless otherwise agreed when consent was given.

All keys must be returned. If all keys are not returned, the Tenant is held liable for the costs of replacing the lock.

The Tenant is obliged to give the Landlord access to hold viewings for both potential tenants and potential buyers during the last 3 months of the tenancy, unless otherwise agreed.

Return is deemed to have taken place when the rental object has been cleaned, is in the same condition as at takeover, and the keys have been handed over, unless otherwise specifically agreed.

12. Compulsory Eviction

If the rent is not paid when due, and not within 14 days after the Landlord has subsequently sent a payment notice, the Landlord may demand compulsory eviction without a lawsuit, cf. the Norwegian Enforcement Act § 4-18 cf. § 13-2, third paragraph, letter a.

If the tenancy has expired and the Tenant does not move out within 14 days after the Landlord has subsequently sent a notice to vacate, the Landlord may demand compulsory eviction without a lawsuit, cf. the Norwegian Enforcement Act § 4-18 cf. § 13-2, third paragraph, letter b.

If the Landlord cancels the agreement due to material breach, the Tenant is obliged to compensate the loss of rent incurred by the Landlord. Likewise, the Tenant must compensate all costs that the breach causes the Landlord, including legal costs and expenses for clearing and cleaning.

14. The Tenancy Act

In the event of conflict between provisions of this agreement and non-mandatory provisions of the Norwegian Tenancy Act, this agreement takes precedence.

15. Legal Venue

The address of the property is the legal venue in all disputes, unless otherwise agreed.
